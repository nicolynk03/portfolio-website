Honours thesis

My honours project explores the use of machine learning to predict daily patient arrivals in the emergency department. The work extends further by forecasting sub-categories of arrivals, such as ward type and patient complexity. I experimented with models including SARIMAX, LSTM, and Random Forest, while gaining valuable experience in feature engineering to ensure high-quality data for training and testing.
