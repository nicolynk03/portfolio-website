About Me

Hi, my name is Nicolyn — you can call me Nic for short👋. I've always been intrigued by how technology can solve real-world problems, whether it's helping a friend, tackling everyday challenges, or just having fun building cool apps and projects.

Originally from Indonesia 🌏, I pursued Computer Science at the University of Tasmania, Australia. That's where I discovered programming to be both challenging and rewarding - and where I built lasting memories and skills. Since then, I've developed a strong passion for software development, AI, and cybersecurity.

When I'm not working on projects, you'll often find me exploring YouTube tutorials (sometimes learning new languages or random facts), listening to Spotify 🎧, or watching Liverpool Football Club⚽. I'm always eager to lend a hand, especially in the digital world, because I believe technology is a powerful tool to make the world better. Let's connect and build something impactful (and awesome) together!
